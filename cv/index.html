Summary Microbial ecologist studying host–microbe interactions, aquatic microbiomes, and cross-domain ecosystem dynamics. Current work integrates bacterial and eukaryotic community ecology, multi-omics, and field-based freshwater research in the Laurentian Great Lakes.

Projects

  • 2025.10 - Present
    Great Lakes microbial and eukaryotic food web dynamics
    Postdoctoral research integrating bacterial and eukaryotic community data to understand cross-domain ecological dynamics under nutrient and environmental stress regimes in the Laurentian Great Lakes.
    • Connects microbial ecology with zooplankton and broader freshwater food web processes.
    • Integrates sequencing, field sampling, and ecosystem-scale questions.
  • 2019.01 - 2025.01
    Host-microbe feedbacks in pitcher plant mosquito microecosystems
    Doctoral research investigating how mosquito hosts shape the composition, diversity, and function of environmentally acquired bacterial communities in carnivorous pitcher plant aquatic microecosystems.
    • Examined host-mediated niche construction and microbiota assembly.
    • Linked microbial community structure to host development and ecosystem function.
